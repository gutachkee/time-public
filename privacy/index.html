Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how time ("the App") handles information when you use the App.

Information We Collect

time does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to the developer.

The App does not require you to create an account and does not collect information such as your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or other personal data.

Anything you enter in the App — your display name, your focus items, and your recorded focus sessions — is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere. The App contains no networking code and works entirely offline.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store.

The developer does not receive or store your payment card information. Apple may process information related to your purchases according to Apple's own privacy policies.

Notifications

If you turn on break reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your device. These are generated and delivered entirely on-device; no notification content is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Analytics and Advertising

time does not use third-party advertising services.

The App does not use third-party analytics services, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Data Sharing

Because the App does not collect personal information, the developer does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

The developer does not retain personal information collected through the App.

Because the App does not maintain user accounts or store personal information on developer-operated servers, there is normally no personal data for the developer to delete. Deleting the App from your device removes the data it stored there.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
