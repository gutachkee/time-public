Using the app

I forgot to stop the timer overnight

A run stops on its own once it reaches that item's daily goal, and a session that crosses midnight is split so each day keeps its own share. If a run still ended up longer than it should have, stop it and adjust the item's goal — the recorded history stays as it is.

Why can't I run two focus items at the same time?

That's deliberate. Starting one item pauses whichever was running, so the hours you look back on always describe one thing at a time. There's no setting to turn it off.

The Live Activity or Dynamic Island isn't showing

Check Settings → time → Live Activities on your iPhone, and Settings → Face ID & Passcode → Live Activities if the Lock Screen is the problem. The Dynamic Island only exists on iPhone 14 Pro and later; on other iPhones the same information appears on the Lock Screen instead.

Break reminders never arrive

Break reminders are local notifications, so they need notification permission: Settings → time → Notifications → Allow Notifications. Focus modes and Do Not Disturb will also hold them back unless you allow time through.

A Home Screen widget is stuck on an old number

iOS decides when to refresh widgets, so a small lag is normal. If one looks frozen, open the app once — that nudges a reload. Removing and re-adding the widget clears a genuinely stuck one.

I don't want an item any more, but I want its history

Archive it instead of deleting it. An archived item disappears from the home screen but its recorded sessions stay in your stats and weekly history. Deleting an item removes its sessions with it, and that can't be undone.

What do I need to run time?

An iPhone or iPad on iOS 17 or later — that covers iPhone XS, XR, SE (2nd generation) and newer. iOS 17 is the floor because the app is built on SwiftData and the Observation framework.

Your data

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Everything lives on the device, so it travels with a device transfer or an encrypted iPhone backup (iCloud Backup, or a Finder backup with "Encrypt local backup" ticked). Restoring the new phone from that backup brings your focus items and sessions across. There is no sync and no export, so a fresh install on a new phone starts empty.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app. That removes the database it kept on your device, and there is nothing on any server to ask me to delete — see the Privacy Policy. To clear less than everything, delete individual focus items in the items editor.

Do you see any of my focus data?

No. The app contains no networking code at all: no account, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs. Your items, sessions and settings never leave your device.

Pro, purchases and billing

I bought Pro but the app still shows free limits

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, with the same Apple ID you bought with. If it still doesn't unlock, check that you're signed into that Apple ID in Settings → [your name] → Media & Purchases, then email me the details and I'll help you track it down.

How do I cancel or change the yearly plan?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside time: open apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions, or on your device go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal and Pro stays active until the period you've paid for ends. The lifetime purchase never renews and needs no cancelling.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple, and I can't issue one. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com with the Apple ID you bought with. If something in the app is the reason you want one, tell me too — I'd rather fix it.

Does Pro work on my other devices?

Yes. Pro is tied to your Apple ID, so it follows you to any iPhone or iPad signed into the same account — use Restore Purchases on the second device. Family Sharing is enabled, so your family group is covered too.

What happens to my items if I stop paying?

Nothing you've recorded is lost. The app returns to the free limits — three focus items and the Blush palette — and items beyond that limit simply can't be started again. Their history stays intact and still counts in your stats, and it all comes back if you buy Pro again.

Which features are free and which are Pro?

Free covers three focus items, the Blush palette in Day, Night or Auto, the Live Activity and Lock Screen ring, and every stat, streak and break reminder. Pro raises the limit to eight items and adds all nine palettes with matching app icons, plus the small and medium Home Screen widgets. The full breakdown is on the home page.
